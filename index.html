My Notes

⚠️ Please read before relying on this tool This app has real limits — understanding them could matter for your safety:
  • It can only listen for your codeword while this browser tab is open and your screen is on. It cannot listen with the screen off or the app closed.
  • It cannot control your phone's built-in camera or voice recorder app — no app can do that. Instead, it has its own recorder built in below.
  • No app can send a file directly to 911 — that channel doesn't exist for any app. When recording starts, a one-tap "Call 911" button appears instead — it still requires your tap to actually place the call, which is a deliberate safety choice: automatically completing a call from a possible false trigger could cause real problems.
  • An optional trusted contact can get a pre-filled SOS text (with your location, if allowed) ready to send in one tap — this also requires your final tap to send, for the same reason.
  • Your phone/browser will always show a visible microphone or recording indicator whenever recording is active — this can't be hidden, by design, for your protection against secret surveillance too.
  • All recordings, reports, and your trusted contact are stored only on this device — nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.
  • Recording laws vary by state — most states only require your own consent (you're a participant), but about 12 states require the other person's consent too. Many of those states have specific exceptions for domestic violence situations. If you want certainty for your situation, a local DV advocate or attorney can advise quickly.
  • This is not a substitute for calling emergency services yourself. If you are in immediate danger, call 911 (or your local emergency number) if it is safe to do so.
